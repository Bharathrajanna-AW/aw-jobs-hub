Questions

Good to know

Are these real job openings?

Yes. Every listing comes straight from the company's official careers page, and the Apply button takes you there. Openings can close at any time, so check the company page for the latest status and eligibility.

Does it cost anything?

No. AW Jobs Hub is free. Analytics Wallah never charges for job listings or referrals. If anyone asks you to pay for a job, it's a scam.

How often is the board updated?

Every morning at 7 AM IST, plus extra hand-picked roles from our team. Roles marked "New" appeared since the last update.

Why does one card say "5 openings"?

The company has several openings with the same job title, often for different teams. We show them as one card so the list stays easy to scan. Where the company's site allows it, a "See all openings" link shows each one there.

What does "Experience not stated" mean?

The company didn't mention years of experience in the job description. These roles are often open to early-career applicants, so read the description and apply if you fit.

How does "My skills" work?

Tap My skills and pick what you know, or tap "Fill this from my resume" to tick them automatically. Your resume is read on your own phone or laptop and is never uploaded or stored. Every job then shows how many of its skills you already have, what's missing, and where to learn it.

How do I save roles for later?

Tap the heart on any card. Saved roles stay on this device and browser, and you can see them all with the Saved filter.
